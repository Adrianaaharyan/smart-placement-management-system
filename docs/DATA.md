# Data

## What is official and what is sample

| Data | Where it lives | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placement rate, offers, PPOs, internship offers, recruiters, medians, sector split, package distribution | `js/data/official.js` | Official, copied from KRMU |
| School-wise table for 2024-25 (11 schools) | `js/data/official.js` | Official, copied from KRMU |
| Recruiter names in the seed drives | `js/data/drives.js` | Real names from KRMU's recruiter lists |
| Roles, CTCs and eligibility rules of the seed drives; dates are offsets from the day the sample is generated | `js/data/drives.js` | Example values |
| 120 students, their skills, applications and offers | generated by `js/core/sample.js` | Sample |

KRMU does not publish student-level data and it would be personal data if it did. The sample generator creates a reproducible cohort (the same seed always gives the same students) so every feature can be demonstrated. The app shows a banner on every screen while sample records are loaded.

The official figures are typed into `js/data/official.js`. They do not update when the university updates its page. To refresh them, re-read the sources above and edit that file; `npm test` checks that the school totals still add up to the 2024-25 annual report (1,015 eligible, 816 placed).

## Where the sources disagree

These are shown in the app under "Check before you quote these numbers". Confirm them with the CDC before using any of these figures in an official document.

1. **Stated rates do not match the counts.** Seven of eleven schools state a placement rate more than 3 percentage points away from placed divided by eligible. The largest gaps: Agricultural Sciences (86% stated, 50% computed), Legal Studies (91% and 70%), Medical and Allied Sciences (85% and 70%).
2. **Impossible or repeated CTC values.** The School of Basic and Applied Sciences lists an average CTC of 10.5 LPA above its own maximum of 10 LPA. The Engineering, Education and Basic and Applied Sciences schools carry the same CTC figures, which looks like a copy error.
3. **Highest package.** ₹56.6 LPA in the "At a Glance" section, ₹34 LPA in both annual reports. Some third-party pages cite ₹36 LPA.
4. **Average package.** 4.25 LPA in the 2024-25 report, 6 to 7 LPA in 2025-26.
5. **Cohort label.** The page introduction refers to the 2025-26 cohort, while the school table is labelled 2024-25.
6. **Package band label.** The lowest band of the 2025-26 distribution reads "Above 1 LPA". The app shows it as 1 to 2 LPA and says this is an interpretation.
7. **Totals.** 816 placed of 1,015 eligible is 80.4%. The 2024-25 report states 81%.

## CSV format for real student records

```
name,roll,branch,cgpa,backlogs,skills,aptitude,projects,internships
Aarav Sharma,2201010101,B.Tech CSE,8.1,0,Python;Java;SQL,72,2,1
```

- `skills` are separated by semicolons. Values must not contain commas.
- `aptitude`, `projects` and `internships` are optional (defaults 50, 0 and 0).
- `branch` should match a programme name in `PROGRAMMES` in `js/config.js`. An unknown programme is accepted and added to the filters, but drives only list programmes you name when you create them.
- Rows with fewer than five columns, a missing name or programme, or a CGPA outside 0 to 10 are skipped and counted.

Importing replaces all students and clears all applications. Drives are kept.

Real student records are personal data. Do not commit them to a public repository.
